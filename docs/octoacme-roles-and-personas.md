# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on architecture and design decisions
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** to understand acceptance criteria and testability requirements
- Partner with **UX/Design Leads** to implement usable, accessible features
- Coordinate with **DevOps/Platform Engineers** on deployment and infrastructure needs
- Receive prioritization and context from **Product Managers**

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** to align priorities and business objectives
- Partner with **UX/Design Leads** on user research and usability validation
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on release planning and timeline management
- Engage with **Technical Leads** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Guide **Developers** through clear acceptance criteria and feature specifications

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Stakeholders/Sponsors** on scope, timeline, and business approvals
- Work with **Product Managers** on release planning and backlog prioritization
- Collaborate with **Technical Leads** on technical dependencies and risk mitigation
- Support **QA/Testing Leads** in test planning and acceptance criteria validation
- Partner with **DevOps/Platform Engineers** on deployment scheduling and readiness
- Facilitate communication across all roles

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plan for features and releases
- Own acceptance criteria validation and sign-off
- Identify quality risks and testing gaps
- Collaborate on Definition of Done and quality gates
- Manage test automation and regression testing
- Participate in sprint planning to assess testability

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Reduce regression and post-release incidents
- Provide early feedback on testability and design
- Establish trust through thorough validation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- QA sign-off in PR reviews and release checklists
- Test results and quality metrics in status updates
- Defect triage and risk escalation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** to validate implementations and identify defects
- Work with **Product Managers** to clarify acceptance criteria and priority of testing
- Collaborate with **UX/Design Leads** to validate usability and accessibility
- Advise **Technical Leads** on testability of architectural decisions
- Support **Project Managers** in assessing release readiness and risk
- Coordinate with **DevOps/Platform Engineers** on test environment setup and deployment verification

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide system design, technology decisions, and technical risk mitigation. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and major technical components
- Guide technology selections and trade-offs
- Review design documents and architecture decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on technical best practices
- Advocate for code quality, performance, and security

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and system fragility
- Ensure consistency with organizational standards
- Support team growth through mentoring and guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and guidance
- Risk identification and escalation
- Knowledge sharing and best practice documentation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on design decisions and technical best practices
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on feasibility and technology trade-offs
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** on testability and test strategy
- Support **Project Managers** with technical risk identification and mitigation planning
- Partner with **DevOps/Platform Engineers** on infrastructure and deployment considerations
- Advise **UX/Design Leads** on technical constraints affecting user experience

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approvals, and resource authorization. They represent customer needs, business objectives, and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project scope and authorize resource allocation
- Provide guidance on business priorities and trade-offs
- Review and validate project progress and outcomes
- Escalate blockers and organizational risks
- Champion the project internally and externally

### Goals
- Ensure delivered solutions drive business value
- Align projects with organizational strategy
- Remove organizational barriers to delivery
- Maximize return on investment

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Scope and priority decisions
- Risk and blocker escalation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Project Managers** on scope, timeline, and resource approvals
- Partner with **Product Managers** on vision, priorities, and success metrics
- Review outcomes with **Developers** and **QA/Testing Leads** during demos and acceptance
- Provide business context to **Technical Leads** for strategic technology decisions
- Escalate organizational blockers affecting **DevOps/Platform Engineers** and other teams
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business objectives

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX and Design Leads ensure solutions are usable, accessible, and aligned with the design system. They validate user needs and advocate for user-centered design.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and translate findings into requirements
- Design user experiences and validate usability
- Ensure alignment with design system and accessibility standards
- Participate in feature definition and acceptance criteria
- Review implementations for design fidelity
- Identify and escalate usability risks

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that delight users
- Maintain design consistency and accessibility
- Reduce post-launch usability issues
- Drive adoption through intuitive experiences

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and user research findings
- Acceptance criteria and usability validation
- Accessibility and design system guidance
- User feedback and design iteration

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Managers** on user research and solution validation
- Collaborate with **Developers** to ensure design fidelity in implementation
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** on usability testing and accessibility validation
- Advise **Technical Leads** on user experience impact of technical decisions
- Support **Project Managers** with user feedback and design iteration planning
- Communicate design requirements to **Stakeholders/Sponsors** for approval

---

## DevOps / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Platform Engineers own deployment infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and operational reliability. They enable teams to deploy with confidence and monitor systems in production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and infrastructure
- Manage CI/CD tooling and automation
- Ensure system reliability, performance, and security in production
- Support incident response and rollback procedures
- Define deployment standards and best practices
- Collaborate on operational readiness and monitoring

### Goals
- Enable safe, frequent, reliable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and downtime
- Support rapid incident detection and recovery
- Maintain system performance and security

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- CI/CD pipeline configuration and standards
- Operational metrics and incident response
- Infrastructure and reliability escalation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Support **Developers** in understanding deployment pipelines and CI/CD standards
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on deployment scheduling and release windows
- Partner with **Technical Leads** on infrastructure architecture and scalability planning
- Work with **QA/Testing Leads** to set up test environments and verify post-deploy functionality
- Provide **Stakeholders/Sponsors** with uptime and reliability metrics
- Collaborate with all teams on operational readiness and incident response
